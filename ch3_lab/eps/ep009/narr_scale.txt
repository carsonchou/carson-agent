One line on what zero point five seven means, because the rest of this depends on it. Effect size is not whether something is true. It is how far apart two groups are. Around zero point two is small, zero point five is medium, zero point eight is large. Those two numbers sit oddly together. With 61 people, only a fairly large effect could have cleared the significance threshold at all — so a small study reporting zero point five seven is exactly the shape of result that gets through that filter.
