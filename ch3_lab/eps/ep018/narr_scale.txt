Before the second number, one line on what a correlation is. A correlation is how tightly two things move together — not whether one causes the other. Around zero point one is small, zero point three is medium, zero point five is large. Those two numbers sit oddly together. With 37 people, only a fairly large effect could have cleared the significance threshold at all — so a small study reporting minus zero point nine six is exactly the shape of result that gets through that filter.
