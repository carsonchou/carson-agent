One line on what zero point six three means, because the rest of this depends on it. Effect size is not whether something is true. It is how far apart two groups are. Around zero point two is small, zero point five is medium, zero point eight is large. And with only 72 people in the original, that number had a lot of room to move by chance. Small samples do not just give you less certainty — they give you a wider spread of possible answers, in both directions.
