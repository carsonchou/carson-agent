In nineteen ninety-eight, a study asked people to resist a plate of warm cookies. Then it gave them an impossible puzzle. The people who resisted the cookies gave up on the puzzle sooner. The conclusion: willpower is a finite resource. Use it here, and you have less of it there. It was called ego depletion, and it was cited over four thousand times. It shaped self-help books, corporate training, even school policy. Then, in twenty sixteen, twenty-three laboratories ran the experiment again. Same protocol, agreed in advance, registered before a single participant walked in. Two thousand one hundred and forty-one people. The measured effect was zero point zero four. For reference, an effect that small is, in practice, indistinguishable from nothing at all.
